## Answer-key rows requiring review

Approved correction: Level 3 questions 38 and 39 (Excel rows 39 and 40) had their option sets exchanged. The app now assigns the city choices to the Superblocks question (D: Barcelona) and the time choices to the transfer-time question (A, clarified as time changing services including walking and waiting). All other imported questions remain unchanged; the original workbook is unchanged.

These differences are recorded without silently changing the supplied key. Some are equivalent wording; others appear to be stale content or conflicting keys. In particular, Level 5 question 16 marks D, while its question and option B describe emissions per passenger-distance.

- **Level 2, row 49 (question 48):** What area around a transit station does the TOD Policy define as the influence area?
  - Marked A: 500-800m
  - Correct answer field: A five-minute walking radius, about 400 metres
- **Level 3, row 50 (question 49):** What is the primary purpose of form-based codes?
  - Marked A: Regulate building form and public-realm relationships
  - Correct answer field: Actual arrival times and the published timetable
- **Level 4, row 51 (question 50):** Which monitoring plan can show whether a street redesign achieved its intended results?
  - Marked D: Pre- and post physical conditions, street use, and impacts assessment
  - Correct answer field: Compare physical conditions, street use, and impacts before and after
- **Level 5, row 16 (question 15):** In transport decarbonisation, what does the Avoid–Shift–Improve framework prioritise under Avoid?
  - Marked A: Reducing the need for motorised travel
  - Correct answer field: Successive buses arriving together after a long gap
- **Level 5, row 17 (question 16):** What does Transport Carbon Intensity expressed in gCO₂e/passenger-km measure?
  - Marked D: Emissions per kilometre of road
  - Correct answer field: Power rate of 120 kW; energy amount of 240 kWh
- **Level 5, row 19 (question 18):** Which planning authority developed Navi Mumbai as a planned satellite city?
  - Marked A: City and Industrial Development Corporation (CIDCO)
  - Correct answer field: A distance- or zone-based transit fare
- **Level 5, row 26 (question 25):** Which city introduced the world's first congestion charging zone in 1975?
  - Marked D: Singapore
  - Correct answer field: Routine vehicle operation and maintenance
- **Level 5, row 39 (question 38):** A bus corridor has high ridership but unreliable travel times. Which intervention should be prioritised?
  - Marked C: Bus priority and signal management
  - Correct answer field: Scheduled terminal recovery time
- **Level 5, row 50 (question 49):** Which parking occupancy range is commonly targeted under demand-responsive pricing?
  - Marked D: 75–85%
  - Correct answer field: Compare before and after against a similar untreated corridor
- **Level 6, row 24 (question 23):** Why must an accessible toilet's emergency alarm cord extend close to the floor?
  - Marked C: Allow a fallen person to call for help
  - Correct answer field: Allow someone who has fallen to call for help
